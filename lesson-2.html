KNIGHT BUS

The purple triple-decker that turns up out of nowhere when a stranded wizard sticks their wand out.

Say its name out loud.

You said “nite bus”. So where did the K go?

The secret

Nowhere. People just stopped saying it.

About 700 years ago, everybody pronounced words like that with the k right out loud. Knight sounded roughly like this:

K-NI-CH-T

Every letter got said. Over hundreds of years people's mouths got lazier and the sounds dropped off — but nobody ever went back and changed the spelling.

So silent letters aren't a mistake and they aren't there to catch you out. They're a fossil — the old way of saying it, still stuck on the page.

And that's useful. Say the old version in your head while you write, and you'll say every letter you need.

⚰️ mb — Dumbledore’s tomb, by the lake.

Tap each one. It'll show you how it used to sound. Say that version out loud. It sounds ridiculous — that's how you know you're doing it right.
