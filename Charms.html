The Standard Book of Spells

Say a sentence out loud. It breaks into pieces. Put it back together in the right order — because in Charms, the order is the whole spell. It's Levi-O-sa, not Levio-SAR.

Step one

Say your sentence

Sofia says it out loud. Whoever is helping types it in exactly as she said it — mistakes and all.

Step two

Put it back in order

Tap a word to place it, or drag it where you want it. Tap a placed word to take it back.
Clue: one word has a capital letter. That is what capitals are for — they tell you where a sentence begins.

For the tutor. This is not a spelling activity — it is a holding activity. Sofia can compose a good sentence out loud and then lose half of it on the way to the page. The cards keep the sentence still while she works on the order, the capital letter and the full stop.
