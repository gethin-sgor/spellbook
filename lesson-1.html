The secret
💀
MORT

= death

Which is a good name for him. But here's the bit that matters for you — once you know that brick, you get all of these for free:

mortal = can die
immortal = not death
mortuary = where the bodies go

Immortal literally means not death. That is the exact thing he spends seven books trying to become — and it was written inside his name the whole time.

You never have to remember how to spell those three words separately. They're all just mort with something stuck on.

Worth knowing

This is why some words never made sense

Look at these. They all end the same way and it never seems to follow any rule:

conduct   product   instruct
construct   aqueduct

That's because "uct" was never a sound. It's not a spelling pattern and it never was one.

It's duct — the same brick as in Reducto. It means to lead.

An aqueduct leads water. A conductor leads an orchestra. You don't sound those words out — you build them.
